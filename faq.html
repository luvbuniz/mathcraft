ANSWERS FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY

A few things you might wonder.

Getting started, saved games, parent sign-in, and what to expect from the beta.

What grades is Stackadoo for?

The new game supports Kindergarten through Grade 5. Choose a grade for each player when you start a New Adventure. Kindergarten has a simplified experience.

Do we need an account?

No. You can start playing in this browser. Parent sign-in is optional and supports Google, an emailed sign-in link, and existing passwords. Children use player nicknames.

Will signing in automatically bring back our old games?

Signing in alone does not move games. Choose Bring an old game, then select the players to copy. If you previously backed up to your parent account, sign in and choose Check my cloud backup.

Will our old worlds and learning records be kept?

The transfer makes a copy and keeps the original saves in place. Learning records and the original source details are retained. Some old belongings do not yet have playable matches in the new game, so check the transferred player before changing over.

Why do I see different players on different devices?

Games saved only in a browser belong to that browser and device. Use the same browser and website address you used before, or check an existing parent-account cloud backup. New beta progress does not yet sync between devices.

Why does the game take time to open?

The first visit downloads the 3D game and artwork. A stable connection helps; later visits can reuse cached files. This beta currently has a large initial download, and loading performance is still being improved.

Can we play offline?

Please use an internet connection for this web beta. Offline play has not yet been verified for the new browser version.

How do we unlock heroes?

Jack and Emma are available from the start. More heroes unlock as correct answers add up. Queen Fox starts at 25 correct answers and Dragon at 50. You do not need a perfect streak.

How do I open the grown-ups area?

Hold the grown-ups button for three seconds. If you have set a parent PIN, enter it. Normal parent entry no longer asks a multiplication question.

Can we turn the music off?

Yes. Use the music control on the welcome screen, New Adventure screen, or in the game.

I forgot my sign-in. What should I do?

Open Sign in, then Trouble signing in. Request a fresh email sign-in link or a password reset. If you used Google, choose the same Google account.

Can we still play the classic game?

Yes! Kids can still play the classic Stackadoo game any time at stackadoo.com/old, and it’s still in the Android app. Their classic worlds stay right where they left them, so they can visit both.
