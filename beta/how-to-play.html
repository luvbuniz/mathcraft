ONE SMALL STEP. ONE BIG IDEA.

Your first adventure starts here.

You do not need a long tutorial to get going. Try one question and see what you can build.

  1. Choose your player

    Pick New Adventure, ask a grown-up to help choose your grade, and select Jack or Emma.

  2. Tap a question box

    Start in Math Meadow. A correct answer earns rewards you can use in your world.

  3. Build something yours

    Open Menu → Build to begin building. Try the camera controls to find the view you like.

  4. Explore and make it home

    Visit the shops. Your Bag holds the things you own, including furniture for your home.

  5. Follow the train to Reading Town

    Visit the station when you are ready to explore reading, spelling, and grammar.

  6. Ask King Lion for help

    Use the game’s help when you need it. Grown-ups can check learning records in the parent area.
