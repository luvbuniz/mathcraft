LEARNING GOES PLACES

Two towns. So many possibilities.

Math Meadow and Reading Town bring learning and world-building into the same adventure.

Math Meadow

Every adventure begins here. Answer math questions, collect rewards, and turn your ideas into a world you can walk around.

  • Choose Kindergarten through Grade 5 when setting up a player.
  • Earn bricks and money by answering questions.
  • Explore the shops, furnish a home, or start building your own.

Reading Town

Hop on the train and discover a town built around words, stories, and imagination.

  • Practise reading, spelling, and grammar.
  • Visit the library and explore stories.
  • Try the Spelling Bee and Grammar Cinema.
